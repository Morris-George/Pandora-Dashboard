ZONE STOCK (/vm/stock, api/_zonestock.js via /api/zonestock -> /api/vm?zonestock=1)

DAILY STORE REPORT UPDATE (typed in on the site, twice a day)

- Storage (api/_daily.js, op=shift): rec.shifts[date] = {m:{v,t,q,c,x,by,at}, n:{...}}; rec.rows[date] is always worked out from the shifts (sales = cash+visa, morning/night sales = each shift's cash+visa). A store that starts mid-month is compared with the target from its first day.
- Each month starts fresh: the page opens on the current month, earlier months stay in the Month filter. The first shift of a new month copies the previous month's target and each weekday's share of it (rec.carried = true, shown as a note); area managers and the super admin change it with "Set the month target" (op=target: month target + a share per weekday). - The Excel upload is still there (small link at the bottom of the entry box) for numbers already in the old sheet; imported days replace typed shifts of the same day and show "From Excel".
- Remove Excel data: the Excel box has 'Remove all days that came from Excel' (area managers and super admin; op=clearimport) which deletes every day that has numbers but no typed shifts, in the stores they may open. Typed shifts, targets and weekday shares stay.
- Sales people and store managers see no store dropdowns: their store is a fixed label on the entry box; the server refuses any other store (shifts, reads, delete).
