Staff approvals

Sales people create their own account. Nobody sees any sales until you approve and link them to the right sales person.

Forgot password requests

These people asked for a new password. Call the number to check it is really them, then set a new password and send it to them.

Waiting for approval

Choose who this person is in the sales file, then press Approve.

Active people

Everyone who can sign in right now. Use the buttons to show only staff, area managers, finance or the brand manager.

Special accounts

For one person who needs a different report, for example the Visual Merchandiser. They sign in with their own username and password and open only what you give them.
Send the username and password to the person yourself. You can change the password here any time.

Refill Order, Slow Moving and Distribution access

Refill Order (also opens Slow Moving). View: sees the order. Input: also types Team Feedback and submits. Manage: also uploads the files, syncs ERP, changes the settings and approves.
Orders Distribution. Warehouse: scans the shipment and downloads the store lists. Manage: also uploads invoices, sets store priority and changes the split. You always have Manage on both.

Switched off

They cannot sign in. You can switch them on again.
