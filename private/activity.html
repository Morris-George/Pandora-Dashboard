About this page

It records sign-ins, wrong passwords, the pages people open (and the time a page is actually in use: the page is on screen and being touched), and every change made on the site. It never records passwords, and it does not look at what is on a page. The last 300 lines are kept for each person, and daily totals for 150 days. Anyone who is not signed in (a wrong username, a new sign-up, a forgotten password) is shown as "Not signed in". It is a good idea to tell the team the site keeps this log.
